Why this is different

Other tools drop a big word every 3 seconds. We work like an editor: we know when silence works and when a word explodes.

AI direction that understands you

Our AI reads your transcript like an editor: what's the hook, what's the core, what's filler. No more random words getting a spotlight.

Vision analysis per moment

The AI looks at every highlight frame and decides: is the word better placed on water, on the ground, behind the person? Looks like magic.

26 text animations

"flies" gets a speed-trail. "explodes" gets radial impact. "broken" shatters. The AI auto-picks what fits the word.

Music & speech beat

Text pumps on the beat when music plays. On speech onset when it doesn't. Nothing looks random, everything locks in.

Moments editor

See every planned moment with a video-frame preview. Change the effect, animation, or scene. Timeline view shows the structure at a glance.

Two-pass validator

The AI checks its own suggestions. Auxiliary verbs, filler words — they don't make it into the video. Only real core statements.
