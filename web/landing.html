The product

Not a subtitle plugin. An editor that gets what you mean.

It reads your transcript, decides where words should hit hard and where silence works, and renders a finished piece. Nothing is random, and nothing is a fixed template.

01

An AI director, not a template

The AI chooses keywords, effects and camera moves from what you actually say: 9 looks, 26 text animations, beat-synced to your music. Every video is directed fresh.

02

Fix the transcript before it renders

Names, technical terms, brands: you see the full transcript first and correct any word. It only renders once it reads right, so nothing lands on screen misspelled.

03

Sound, emphasis and a hook score

Real licensed sounds instead of synthetic beeps, matching emphasis on the words that matter, and a hook score that rates your opener before you post it.

04

It learns your style

Show it one video whose captions you love. It studies the pacing, emphasis and hook behaviour, and your future renders follow that taste. Every edit teaches it more.

05

It fits into your edit, not the other way round

Take the finished MP4, or take the parts: an SRT subtitle file for YouTube, and a transparent caption layer (ProRes 4444) you drop straight over your original in Premiere or Resolve — same captions, still movable.

06

Motion graphics, same engine

Clean animated graphics in the Apple keynote language — pills, widgets, app-store cards, lower thirds, chat and notification clips. One flat credit each, MP4, ready to cut in.

Common questions

What is a credit?

1 credit renders 1 minute of finished video, billed per started minute. A 90-second clip costs 2 credits. A motion clip is 1 credit flat. Output is up to 1080p — the resolution every short-form platform delivers to viewers anyway.

Do I need a subscription?

No. You buy credits once. There is no plan, no renewal and nothing to cancel.

Do credits expire?

Yes, after 6 months. We say so here and before you pay, and we remind you by email before anything expires.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. There is a 10-second demo without an account, and every confirmed account gets free credits plus 3 free minutes per month (watermarked until your first purchase).

What happens to my videos?

They are private and deleted from the server automatically after 7 days. Download your renders in time.

Which languages work?

Speech is recognised in most languages. Captions are set in any language written in the Latin, Cyrillic or Greek alphabet, plus Chinese, Japanese and Korean — roughly 40 in practice. Arabic, Hebrew, Hindi and Thai are not supported yet; we say so before you spend a credit rather than after.
