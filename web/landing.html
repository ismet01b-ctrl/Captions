Common questions

What is a credit?

1 credit renders 1 minute of finished video, billed per started minute. A 90 second clip costs 2 credits.

Do I need a subscription?

No. You buy credits once. There is no plan, no renewal and nothing to cancel.

Do credits expire?

Yes, after 6 months. We say that here and before you pay, not in the fine print.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. There is a 10 second demo without an account, and every confirmed account gets 2 free credits plus 3 free minutes per month (watermarked until your first purchase).

What happens to my videos?

They are private, and we delete them from the server automatically after 7 days. Download your renders in time.
