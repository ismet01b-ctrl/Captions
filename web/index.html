Text & pacing

Hook, rhythm, language.

How long is the hook?
The first seconds with denser captions, where most viewers decide to stay. "Off" = no hook.
How many words per card?
Fewer = word by word (energetic, Reels look). More = full phrases (calmer, professional).
How many big moments per minute?
Calm = ~2-3/min (business talk). Balanced = ~5-7/min (standard). Energetic = word by word (Reels/Shorts).
Language
Detected automatically from your audio - no need to pick. Supported: English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Turkish, Polish, Russian, Ukrainian, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Chinese and ~35 more.

Style & effects

Font, colors, word effects, blur.

Which font?
Each tile shows the font exactly as it will look in the video.
Caption color world
Auto = sampled from the scene per moment (premium standard). Black = elegant dark look on bright footage. White = high-end on dark footage.
Which word effects may the AI use?
The AI direction picks one of the enabled effects per moment.
Behind you = big word sits behind the person. Letter build-up = word types itself in. From the blur = cinema-title fade-in. Outline only = fine contour, great for numbers. In the scene = text sits IN the video and moves with the camera.
Blur the background (cinema bokeh)
During big moments the background goes soft while person + text stay sharp, like an interview at open aperture.
How precisely is the person cut out?
Needed so text can run behind them. High = recommended. Maximum = every strand of hair, costs render time.

Camera & output

Motion and resolution.

How much artificial camera motion?
Zooms, pans, push-ins. 0 = fully off (static video), 100 = maximum dynamics. Crash zooms and whip pans come with the look.
Resolution of the finished video
1080p = standard (recommended). 720p = mobile-optimized, smaller file.
